# Mezo direct funding programs for builders (as of 2026-11-12)

Method note: web search plus fetches of mezo.org pages. Akindo's event page, supernormal.foundation, and the Alpha Builders docs page were not readable (docs page redirects to /docs/users/). Judging criteria and Wave 3 were not found anywhere.

## 1. Grants, ecosystem fund, accelerator, builder incentives

### Takeaway
Funding is run by the Supernormal Foundation (Mezo's non-profit steward, launched July 2025) through Alpha Builder / Founder programs, hackathons, bounties and a planned Grant Committee. No public grant size, total fund size, application form or named recipients were found. Grants are explicitly "milestone-based" and "not guaranteed".

### Cited Findings
- Supernormal Foundation launched July 14, 2025; it is a community-driven non-profit steward of Mezo. Alpha Builder and future grant programs give milestone-based funding, co-marketing and technical help. The post states no Foundation funding amounts. Apply via supernormal.foundation. — [Mezo blog](https://mezo.org/blog/p/7a444038-07c0-472b-954c-8019ce189e0e)
- Foundation advisory group: Arthur Hayes, Dan Held, Dovey Wan, Will Reeves (Fold). Release names no grant sizes or total funding. — [Chainwire release](https://chainwire.org/2025/07/14/mezo-launches-supernormal-foundation-to-accelerate-bitcoinfi-innovation-and-mezo-ecosystem-growth/)
- Alpha Builder Program (2025): grants, co-marketing, integration help, BitcoinFi Accelerator access. Seeks live dApps with on-chain users and community; security audit required before mainnet; grants not guaranteed; builders get priority access to future grant programs. (Secondhand via search summary of Mezo docs, page itself not fetchable.) — [Mezo Alpha Builders program post](https://blog.mezo.org/mezo-alpha-builders-program-bitcoinfi-foundations)
- 2026 roadmap: continued "Founder Program" for teams deploying production-ready dApps integrating MUSD (funding, technical help, go-to-market); more hackathons; BitcoinFi Accelerator with Draper Associates, Boost VC, Draper Dragon (two prior cycles deployed over $3M); expanded community bounties; a planned Grant Committee to fund ecosystem development and set treasury spending standards. Roadmap items are labelled aspirational. — [Mezo 2026 roadmap](https://mezo.org/blog/mezo-2026-roadmap)
- Accelerator: at the July 2025 launch, 15 BitcoinFi Accelerator teams had raised a combined $2.25M led by Draper Associates and Boost VC (money from VCs, not the Foundation). — [Mezo blog](https://mezo.org/blog/p/7a444038-07c0-472b-954c-8019ce189e0e)
- Buildathon post names two Foundation follow-on tracks: Mezo Ignition Program (early projects, prototype to mainnet) and Mezo Founder Program (working products with initial traction); selected teams may get milestone grants, integration help, user reach. No eligibility details or amounts given. — [Mezo Buildathon post](https://mezo.org/blog/mezo-buildathon-is-underway)
- Mezo Academy (Foundation, Class of 2026): 4M veMEZO shared over 8 epochs among wallets that lock MEZO / boost veBTC; no application, aimed at holders, not builders. Relevant because Feasy's veMEZO-boost loop could earn it as a wallet. — [Mezo Academy](https://mezo.org/blog/mezo-academy/)

### Inferences
- Ignition and Founder programs are the likeliest route from buildathon to money; Feasy (testnet, working product) maps to Ignition, or Founder once it has mainnet traction. Inferred, not stated.
- Alpha Builder's audit-before-mainnet requirement likely applies to Feasy.

### Gaps
- Grant amounts, total fund size, application form, who decides, past grant recipients: not found.
- Whether Alpha Builder is still open in Nov 2026: unknown.
- Grant Committee status: only a roadmap intention.

## 2. Current buildathon structure (Mezo Buildathon on AKINDO)

### Takeaway
Small prize pool ($7,000 in MEZO total over two waves), paid in MEZO, with the real upside being Foundation follow-on programs. Wave 3 is not described anywhere I could find.

### Cited Findings
- Wave 1: Oct 16-26, $2,000 in MEZO. Wave 2: Nov 2-15, $5,000 in MEZO, final submissions due Nov 15. Winners announced Nov 23 with an online demo session. Eligible Wave 1 projects advance to Wave 2. — [Mezo Buildathon post](https://mezo.org/blog/mezo-buildathon-is-underway)
- Tracks: DeFi (borrowing, lending, looping, yield, auto-compounding vaults) and Access & Distribution. Must integrate MUSD and/or MEZO with working deployment; Mezo, Ethereum, Base, BSC and Mezo testnet accepted; new or extended projects OK. Submission via AKINDO; questions via Mezo Discord. — same post
- Page does not state judging criteria, Wave 3, KYB, or per-wave prize splits. — same post
- A third-party roundup (via search, unverified) says initial Wave 2 submissions due Nov 14, up to 20 shortlisted teams announced Nov 16, develop until Nov 21, live presentations Nov 22; says winners need KYB. Conflicts with official Nov 15/Nov 23 dates; treat as unverified. — [X roundup](https://x.com/_HEISEMMA/status/2117244375776248164) (page itself not fetchable)
- Separate Encode Club x Mezo 6-week hackathon (2026, undated): tracks Bitcoin, MUSD, MEZO; per track 1st $5,000 veMEZO + $5,000 veMEZO milestone grants, 2nd $3,000 + $3,000, Community Choice $2,000 + $2,000; total $30,000 veMEZO pool; must integrate both MUSD and MEZO, testnet demo; KYB before payout; mentorship and visibility from Mezo. Its "read next" links to the Buildathon, so it is a different program. — [Mezo hackathon post](https://mezo.org/blog/the-mezo-hackathon-is-back/)
- Past hackathons: first drew nearly 400 participants and paid 37,500 MUSD across nine winners ([roadmap](https://mezo.org/blog/mezo-2026-roadmap)). Hackathon 2 winners picked by Mezo team on demo quality, code, testnet deployment, protocol integration; community jury for community award; winners get "follow-on grant support" but no amounts given. Winners incl. Cermin, Mezo TreasuryOS, Cark, MezoPay, Mezoir, BynD. — [Hackathon 2 winners](https://mezo.org/blog/announcing-mezo-hackathon-2-winners/)

### Inferences
- Hackathon 2's stated criteria (demo, code, deployment, protocol integration) are probably similar for the Buildathon, but this is an inference. The Encode "MEZO track" (make MEZO productive via staking/governance/liquidity) matches what Wave 1 judges asked of Feasy.

### Gaps
- Wave 3 rewards or existence, judging rubric, KYB for the Buildathon, prize split: not found. Akindo page not readable.

## 5. MEZO tokenomics and access for apps

### Cited Findings
- 1B genesis supply: Community 40% (ecosystem participants, airdrop, dApp LP incentives, validators, grants), Investors & Partners 30%, Team 20%, Foundation 10% (partnerships, ecosystem development, reserves; unlocked at TGE). Team and investors have a 12-month cliff. — [Introducing MEZO](https://mezo.org/blog/introducing-mezo)
- Emissions: about 4.8M MEZO per week, halving roughly every two years. Chain Splitter default 20% validators, 80% ecosystem; Ecosystem Splitter 90% staking gauges, 10% non-staking gauges (about 8% net) that fund grants, partner protocols and development without needing LP. Within branches, veBTC holders vote gauges; veMEZO boosts veBTC up to 5x. — same post
- Staking gauges (about 72% net) pay LPs and MUSD savers staking receipt tokens. This is how Feasy's sMUSD gauge staking earns MEZO. — same post
- Airdrop is 5% of supply across phases; Phase 1 sent 19,824,782 MEZO to 11,845 addresses. — [Allocation methodology](https://mezo.org/blog/the-mezo-allocation-methodology-phase-1)
- Pre-deposit vaults "Bring Bitcoin Home" allocated up to 2.5% of supply to early depositors (secondhand via search summary). — [finanzen.net release](https://www.finanzen.net/nachricht/aktien/mezo-opens-pre-deposit-vaults-to-migrate-bitcoin-liquidity-from-ethereum-back-to-bitcoin-native-infrastructure-15410512)

### Inferences
- An app gets MEZO emissions by having its own gauge that veBTC voters (bribes/votes) point at, or via the non-staking ecosystem gauge route; how a gauge is added or approved was not found.

### Gaps
- Process for an app to get a gauge or ecosystem-gauge allocation; size of the Foundation grant treasury; TGE date conflicts among third-party trackers (official table vs Thesis post), not resolved.
