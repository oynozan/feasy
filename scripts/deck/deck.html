The idea

Deposit BTC, get more BTC.

The vault borrows dollars against the pooled BTC and saves them. Savings income above the loan cost is swapped back into BTC.

No leverage on BTC Fee only on profit fBTC, an ERC-4626 share
How it works

One loop. Anyone can push each step.

1 Deposit Send BTC, get fBTC shares at the current price.
2 Borrow MUSD against the pool at 250% collateral.
3 Save Into Mezo's savings vault, a share staked for MEZO.
4 Harvest Income above the loan cost is swapped to BTC.
5 Unlock Profit reaches the share price over 7 days.
veBTC sleeve 5% of the BTC sits in a four-week veBTC ladder voting for the sMUSD gauge. Fees go to savings, rebates come back as BTC.
The Mezo loop

Every Mezo primitive, working for the same BTC.

BTC The pool's BTC backs one Mezo trove.

veBTC and the boost
The pool's veBTC votes for the same sMUSD gauge, so the staked sMUSD's yield comes back as fees. veMEZO boosts those locks through Mezo's BoostVoter.

Risk controls

Plain BTC exposure, no leverage.

Drop to liquidation 56% BTC fall needed from the 250% target, with nobody rebalancing.
Collateral target 250% Held in a 200 to 300% band, checked on every rebalance.
Profit unlock 7 days Joining just before a payout earns nothing extra.
Owner timelock 24 h Every change is public for a day. Nobody can move user funds.
Spread guard rs > rb Borrowing stops on chain when sMUSD pays less than the loan costs.

Business model

One fee: 10% of the profit.

Nothing on deposits, withdrawals or assets held. The fee only applies when the share price is above its previous high, so nobody pays on a loss or on earning one back.

Revenue grows with TVL and with Mezo's savings rate. Today's sMUSD income carries about 75 to 280 BTC at a 1-point spread, and the guard stops borrowing past that.
