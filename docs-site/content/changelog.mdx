## Liquid veBTC, Nov 12, 2026

A second product, open to anyone: [Liquid veBTC](/liquid-locker). BTC or a veBTC lock comes in as fveBTC, a token that earns Mezo's gauge fees and redeems within four weeks. MEZO or a veMEZO lock comes in as fveMEZO, one permanent lock that boosts every Feasy veBTC lock and takes a share of what they earn.

## v8, Nov 10, 2026

The live vault, [`0x5e78…9Ed2`](https://explorer.test.mezo.org/address/0x5e7800fB24651038e72ad1e055A2B2AD81a59Ed2) on Mezo testnet. It has borrowed since Nov 11.

### New

| Change | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Spread guard](/spread-guard) | No new debt while sMUSD pays less than the loan costs, measured from sMUSD's yield index |
| [Recovery Mode unwind](/recovery-mode) | Repays to Mezo's minimum loan during Recovery Mode, then closes the loan once Mezo allows |
| [Performance fee](/business-model) | 10% of profit above the previous high, capped at 20%. A rise waits 30 days |
| [Savings sleeve](/mezo-loop) | Holds the sMUSD and stakes a share of it in Mezo's sMUSD gauge for MEZO |
| [Booster](/mezo-loop#the-booster) | A permanent veMEZO lock that boosts the vault's veBTC through Mezo's BoostVoter |
| [veBTC sleeve](/vebtc-sleeve) | Checkpoints veBTC before it collects, so a lock's rebate is never missed |
